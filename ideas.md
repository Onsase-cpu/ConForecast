## Chosen approach: Tidal Tactile

### Design Movement

Tidal Tactile draws on **neo-claymorphism** and contemporary meteorological field instruments. It pairs physical, rounded ceramic controls with deliberate open space and thin, precise data marks.

### Core Principles

1. **Forecast first:** The current condition and next useful decision are always dominant.
2. **Tactile calm:** Panels feel gently pressed or raised through soft, cool shadows instead of heavy borders.
3. **Environmental color roles:** Blue communicates weather and navigation, while green denotes favorable or active natural conditions.
4. **Structured softness:** Information uses a fluid, asymmetric current-and-detail layout rather than a uniform dashboard grid.

### Color Philosophy

The base is an off-white sky canvas so the application feels usable in daylight. Clean lake blue is reserved for weather states, actions, and selected moments; leaf green denotes calm, favorable conditions, and environmental metrics. Pale aqua and celadon shadows create material depth without drifting into decoration.

### Layout Paradigm

The home view uses a **weather current**: a broad left-hand conditions canvas flows into a narrower right-hand atmospheric detail bank. A horizontally moving hourly weather strip breaks the main column, while daily outlook cards progress like forecast markers rather than a rigid equal grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A **weather orb** with an offset sun/cloud/rain glyph sits inside the lead conditions card.
2. Small **current bars** of blue and green precede live readings, selected navigation, and timeline markers.
3. A **pressure seam**—a fine multi-tone rule with a soft inset shadow—separates major forecast sections.

### Interaction Philosophy

Search behaves like a direct location instrument: type to surface choices, then select a place to immediately re-center the forecast. Unit choices and saved places remain visible but subordinate; every interaction gets concise confirmation and retains keyboard access.

### Animation

The weather orb breathes subtly with an 8-second opacity/transform loop. Forecast tiles lift by 2px with a 160ms custom ease-out on hover; selected states settle by color and shadow rather than dramatic movement. Lists cascade on first arrival at 40ms intervals. Reduced-motion users receive static states.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** provides measured, modern weather numerals and headlines; **DM Sans** provides clear labels and forecasts. Temperatures use tight tracking and a large, calm scale. Labels are compact, uppercase, and spaced to mimic an instrument face.

### Brand Essence

**Weather, made tangible for people who want a clear read on the day before it begins.** Personality: **calm, precise, restorative**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are confident and observant; CTAs are brief, concrete, and location-led. Avoid filler and alarmism.

> “A gentler read on the day ahead.”

> “Set this place as your weather home.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The product is named **Conforecast**. Its mark is a simple blue-to-green split orb, cut with a slim white current line and paired with a custom-spaced Conforecast wordmark that gently tightens the syllables around the central current mark.

### Signature Brand Color

**Current Blue — #2679D8**

## Style Decisions

- The lead forecast uses an oversized Space Grotesk temperature and weather orb. Search, favorites, and atmospheric detail never outrank this first-read condition.
- Conforecast’s split blue-to-green current is repeated in the logo, section labels, seams, live reading chips, weather-orb marker, and forecast timelines.
- Daily and hourly forecast information uses staggered marker rhythm, technical seams, and thin data traces rather than equal-weight dashboard tiles.
